## 摘要
现代经济面板数据集通常具有高维特征：它们包含大量控制变量的信息，其数量甚至可能超过样本量。然而，关于高维面板计量经济学方法的文献相当有限。在本文中，我们研究具有交互固定效应的高维面板模型，其中回归函数具有加性结构，即每个协变量通过一个未知的非线性分量函数进入模型。我们在此加性框架下开发了估计方法并建立了理论，这显著扩展了Ruecker等人（2025）先前关于高维线性情形的工作。在本文的理论部分，我们推导了我们的估计量在小T和大T面板情形下的收敛速率。该理论辅以全面的蒙特卡罗实验和一项实证应用。

## Abstract
Modern economic panel data sets are often high-dimensional: they contain information on a wide variety of control variables whose number may even exceed the sample size. Nevertheless, the literature on econometric methods for high-dimensional panels is quite limited. In this paper, we study high-dimensional panel models with interactive fixed effects where the regression function has an additive structure, i.e., each covariate enters the model via an unknown nonlinear component function. We develop estimation methodology and theory in this additive framework which substantially extends previous work on the high-dimensional linear case by Ruecker et al. (2025). In the theoretical part of the paper, we derive the convergence rate of our estimator for both the small-T and the large-T panel case. The theory is complemented by comprehensive Monte Carlo experiments and an empirical application.
